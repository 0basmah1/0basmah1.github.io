Planty

Planty is a mobile UI/UX concept designed to solve the common problem of plant neglect due to inconsistent watering schedules.
The goal was to create a high-visibility interface that provides users with immediate visual feedback on the health and needs of their indoor garden.

Key Design Features :
Visual Urgency System: I implemented a color-coded progress bar (Red, Orange, Green) that allows users to scan their entire garden in seconds.
This provides an instant "at-a-glance" understanding of which plants require immediate attention.
Contextual Care Tags: Each plant card includes a "Water Needs" tag (Low, Moderate, High). This categorizes plants by their natural care level,
helping users prioritize tasks based on the plant species' specific requirements.
Interactive Gratification: When the water drop icon is pressed, the progress bar dynamically fills and changes color using Smart Animate transitions.
This provides the user with a satisfying sense of accomplishment and reinforces positive care habits.
Proactive Notification Center: I designed a dedicated Notifications Feed that uses status icons (Happy, Sad, Neutral) and time-stamps.
This ensures users are alerted to "Thirsty" plants and provides a log of recent care activities like "You just watered your plant!"
Nature-Inspired Aesthetic: A soft mint-green and white palette was chosen to evoke a sense of calm and nature.
This aligns the visual brand with the therapeutic activity of gardening while maintaining a clean modern feel.
